# Anna Diyanova: From Question to Product

An 18-second motion film using the portfolio's Formula typography and actual project images.
This is an isolated experiment; the live homepage is not changed.

## Narrative

- 0.00-3.65: Every product starts with a question.
- 3.65-7.40: I turn complexity into clarity. Scattered modules form a coherent system.
- 7.40-13.55: From first idea to real products. Family Subscription, Taxi Booking, Bridge.Travel.
- 13.55-18.00: Anna Diyanova. Founding Product Designer. Portfolio address.

Soft directional wipes connect the scenes. The final title holds for readability.
The requested title "Founding Product Designer" is creative copy, not an independently verified employment title.

## Files

- `Anna-Diyanova-18s.mp4`: Full HD, 60 fps, original synthesized music and transition sounds.
- `Anna-Diyanova-18s-Silent.mp4`: silent version for potential website use.
- `poster.jpg`: still for a video poster.
- `storyboard.jpg`: eight-frame overview.
- `render_intro.py`: reproducible source. Requires Pillow, NumPy and FFmpeg with libx264.

```sh
python3 render_intro.py --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg
python3 render_intro.py --storyboard-only
```

No voice-over, stock music or third-party video footage is used.
